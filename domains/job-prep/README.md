# Job preparation

Prepare a specific application: decompose the role, rehearse the rounds, and
leave with a dated plan.

## Use when

The user is preparing for a job, internship or interview, planning a search, or
facing a take-home. Resume rewriting belongs to the resume module; use it first
when the document is the blocker.

## Inputs

- the role and company, plus the job description when available
- interview stage and date, if known
- the resume or profile, as the only source of experience to speak from
- known weak areas, and the format (onsite, remote, take-home, group)

## Workflow

1. **Decompose the posting.** Requirements in the order the posting emphasises,
   split into technical, behavioural and domain.
2. **Build the competency matrix.** Requirement, story that proves it, strength,
   and what to prepare when nothing proves it.
3. **Write answer skeletons.** One per likely question, built from the user's
   real projects with the STAR shape in `references/methodology.md`. Mark gaps
   instead of inventing details.
4. **Plan the rounds.** For each stage: what is being tested, the three drills
   that train it, and the date each drill runs.
5. **Rehearse.** Ask the user to answer out loud, then critique structure and
   specificity — never supply facts they do not have.
6. **Prepare the close.** Questions to ask the interviewer, and the points the
   user wants remembered.

## Deliverable

1. Competency matrix with strengths and gaps.
2. Answer skeletons for the ten most likely questions, each linked to a real
   project from the user's background.
3. A dated preparation plan: drill, time box, and the output of each drill.
4. Questions to ask, and a short list of facts to confirm about the role.
5. For a take-home: a scoping plan with a stop time and what to cut first.

## Quality bar

- Every answer skeleton traces to a project, role or course the user actually
  described.
- Drills produce something inspectable: a timed answer, a written outline, a
  working solution.
- The plan fits the days remaining before the interview; if it does not, cut
  scope and say what was cut.
- No advice that only works for a different seniority level than the target.

## Out of scope

Offer negotiation beyond preparation for the conversation, visa advice, and
company research that requires browsing the live web — ask the user for the
material instead.
