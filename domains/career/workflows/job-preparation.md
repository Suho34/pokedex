# Job preparation

Prepare a specific application: decompose the role, rehearse the rounds, and
leave with a dated plan.

## Use when

The user names a role, company, interview date, internship or take-home. For
direction with no specific role, use `planning.md`.

## Inputs

The role and company, the posting when available, the stage and date, the resume
or profile as the only source of experience, known weak areas, and the format
(onsite, remote, take-home, group).

## Steps

1. **Decompose the posting.** Requirements in the order the posting emphasises,
   split into technical, behavioural and domain.
2. **Build the competency matrix.** Requirement, story that proves it, strength,
   what to prepare when nothing proves it.
3. **Write answer skeletons.** One per likely question, from the user's real
   projects, in the STAR shape in
   `../references/job-preparation-methodology.md`. Mark gaps instead of inventing
   details.
4. **Plan the rounds.** For each stage: what is tested, three drills, and the date
   each drill runs.
5. **Rehearse.** Ask the user to answer aloud, then critique structure and
   specificity. Never supply facts they do not have.
6. **Prepare the close.** Questions to ask, and the points the user wants
   remembered.

## Deliverable

1. Competency matrix with strengths and gaps.
2. Answer skeletons for the ten most likely questions, each linked to a real
   project.
3. A dated drill plan: drill, time box, output.
4. Questions to ask, and facts to confirm about the role.
5. For a take-home: a scoping plan with a stop time and a cut list.

## Quality bar

- Every skeleton traces to a project, role or course the user described.
- Drills produce something inspectable: a timed answer, a written outline, a
  working solution.
- The plan fits the days remaining; if not, cut scope and say what was cut.
- Nothing assumes a different seniority than the target.

## Out of scope

Negotiation beyond preparing the conversation, visa advice, and live company
research — ask the user for the material instead.
