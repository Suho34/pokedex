# Career planning

Turn a vague ambition into a dated plan with proof artifacts, built from what the
user has actually done.

## Use when

The user asks where to go next: target role, promotion, pivot, skill gaps, "what
should I learn", "where should I be in two years". For a specific application or
interview, use `job-preparation.md`.

## Inputs

Current role and dates; target role or outcome and when; what is provable today;
hours per week; money; location, work authorisation, notice period; risk
appetite (stay and grow, or leave).

## Steps

1. **Audit evidence.** Each claim next to the artifact that proves it. Claims
   without artifacts are marked unproven.
2. **Define the target concretely.** One role title, one seniority level, one
   market, one date.
3. **Reverse-engineer requirements.** Use 8–10 real postings for the target and
   extract the recurring requirements — see `../references/planning-methodology.md`.
4. **Build the gap table.** Requirement, current evidence, gap size, cheapest
   proof, effort in hours.
5. **Sequence the plan.** 90 days, 12 months, 24 months, each item naming its
   artifact and its date.
6. **Set the cadence.** A monthly review and two or three decision points with
   explicit criteria.

## Deliverable

1. Target statement: role, level, market, date.
2. Gap table sorted by requirement frequency, with effort estimates.
3. Sequenced plan, quarter by quarter, each item with artifact and hours.
4. Decision points with numeric criteria.
5. The first action for this week.

## Quality bar

- Every plan item produces something another person can inspect.
- The weekly hours match the user's stated availability. If not, cut scope and
  say what was cut.
- No certificate-only items unless the collected postings actually filter on it.
- The plan states what gets dropped first when a week is missed.

## Out of scope

Salary negotiation scripts (`job-preparation.md`), resume rewrites (`resume`
domain), and predictions about hiring markets beyond the collected postings.
